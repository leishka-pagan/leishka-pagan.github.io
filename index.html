About

From operations to security

I bring nine years of experience across enterprise infrastructure, identity and access management, public-safety operations, automation, and cybersecurity.

At Spectrum, I administered access for more than 5,400 users and became an escalation point for authentication and operational workflow failures. At Metro Net Fire Authority, I supported controlled 911 records workflows and rebuilt a manual quality-assurance process that reduced weekly processing time by approximately 75%.

Those roles shaped how I approach security today. I look beyond whether a control exists and ask whether it works under operational pressure, produces useful evidence, and supports the people responsible for the outcome.

I now apply that approach across cloud security, investigations, security automation, and AI assurance. Lumen, ClearStep, Lantern, Sentinel, and AXIOM demonstrate how I translate risk into enforceable controls using code, policy, evidence, automation, and human oversight.

Cybersecurity matters to me because technical failures have human consequences. My goal is to build systems that interrupt fraud earlier, adapt as threats evolve, and give authorized investigators better evidence.

03Deterministic safety controls for AI applications

Lantern Safety Core

Deterministic safety controls execute before model access.

Lantern Safety Core places deterministic crisis handling ahead of the provider-backed path. Crisis messages receive the 988 resource without requiring an API key, provider client, authentication, or model call. Other requests enter a controlled path with authentication, per-credential rate limiting, a daily provider-call cap, and a request timeout. The system is designed for controlled review, not public production.
